## 1. GLOBAL ART DIRECTION & UNIVERSE RULES

### 1.1 Visual Philosophy
*LITTLE DREAMERS* is set in a vibrant, tactile, welcoming 3D preschool animation universe. Every character and environment is designed to evoke warmth, security, curiosity, and wonder. The characters must feel alive, touchable, huggable, and distinctly stylized with soft geometric forms.

### 1.2 Proportions & Geometry
- **Head-to-Body Ratio:** Exaggerated preschool proportions (2.0 to 3.0 heads tall for children, 1.8 to 2.5 heads tall for animal companions).
- **Soft Geometry:** Rounded silhouettes, zero sharp aggressive corners, soft bevels on all edges, and chubby toddler anatomy (curved belly, soft dimpled wrists and ankles).
- **Eyes:** Large, glossy, deeply expressive eyes with dark pupils, warm amber/brown irises, luminous dual reflection catchlights, and soft animated eyelids for rich emotional storytelling.

### 1.3 Materiality & Shaders
- **Surfaces:** Smooth stylized clay/vinyl tactile finish with microscopic subsurface scattering (SSS) that catches warm rim light.
- **Hair/Fur:** Stylized sculpted volume with fine groom accents; soft velvet-like nap on animal characters rather than hyper-realistic bristly fur.
- **Clothing:** High-grade stylized cotton and jersey textures with visible, soft macro-stitching details and clean, non-distressed fabrics.

### 1.4 Lighting & Color Palette
- **Lighting:** Warm three-point studio lighting with prominent soft golden rim light and pastel ambient fill. Soft contact shadows only; never harsh or pitch-black occlusions.
- **Color Harmony:** Primary and secondary colors balanced with pastel tones. High saturation but strictly calibrated to avoid garishness or eye strain.

### CHARACTER 1: AARAV

#### 1. Identity & Role
- **Name:** Aarav
- **Age:** 2.5 to 3 years old
- **Archetype:** The Curious Explorer & Group Leader
- **Personality:** Curious, adventurous, friendly, empathetic, energetic, and playful. Aarav loves discovering small bugs, pointing out shapes in the clouds, and leading his friends on gentle backyard expeditions.

#### 2. Exact Appearance Description
A cute, healthy toddler boy with a bright smile, soft wavy chestnut-brown hair with a signature playful front cowlick curl that bobs when he jumps, warm olive-golden skin tone, and enormous expressive chocolate-brown eyes. His cheeks are round and blushed with soft peachy-rose warmth.

#### 3. Clothing & Signature Accessories
- **Top:** Premium short-sleeve sky-blue cotton crewneck T-shirt (`#1E88E5`).
- **Chest Emblem:** A clean, vibrant five-pointed golden-yellow star emblem (`#FFD54F`) with rounded tips permanently printed in the exact optical center of the chest.
- **Bottoms:** Comfortable royal-blue elasticized toddler shorts (`#1565C0`) reaching just above the knee with clean white hem stitching.
- **Footwear:** Colorful red toddler sneakers (`#E53935`) with crisp white rubber toe caps and outsoles (`#FFFFFF`) and golden-yellow laces (`#FDD835`). White ankle socks with a thin blue stripe.

| Shoe Soles/Trim | Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Accent |

#### 5. Relative Height & Proportions
- **Relative Height:** 85 cm (100% toddler baseline scale).
- **Proportions:** 2.8 heads tall. Large round cranium, soft toddler neck, gentle toddler belly curve, chubby wrists and ankles.

#### 6. Facial Features
- **Eyes:** Oversized almond-round brown eyes with dual specular highlights (one main key light spot, one smaller bounce dot).
- **Eyebrows:** Soft rounded brown curved brows capable of high expressive arcs.
- **Nose:** Tiny soft button nose with subtle shading, no sharp nostrils.
- **Mouth:** Soft pink lips (`#E57373`), wide toothless or tiny two-front-teeth preschool smile.
- **Cheeks:** Plump, rounded, with radial soft peach-pink blush (`#FFAB91`, 30% opacity).

#### 7. Voice Characteristics
- **Tone:** Bright, warm, melodious, innocent young toddler boy voice.

#### 8. Animation Movement Guidelines
- **Idle:** Weight shifts from foot to foot with gentle toddler bounce; head tilts inquisitively.
- **Walk:** Classic toddler waddle with arms slightly outward for balance.
- **Run:** Bouncy forward lean with rapid little steps, knees lifting high, star emblem swinging proudly.
- **Dance:** Two-handed fist pumps, joyful jumping on both feet, spinning around and laughing.
- **Sitting:** Sits cross-legged or with legs straight out, hands resting on knees.
