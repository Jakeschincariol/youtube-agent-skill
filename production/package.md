## Description

```
Seven pricing levers for a one-person business — how to raise your prices without losing clients, ranked by how fast each one pays.

Start with lever one: the renewal letter you can send today. No new clients, no extra hours, no mindset work.

The renewal letter wording, the three-option quote, and the full script are in the pinned comment.

CHAPTERS
00:00 The quote you delete and retype
00:54 Lever 1 — The renewal letter
02:13 Lever 2 — Sell the date
03:10 Lever 3 — Three prices beat one
04:00 Lever 4 — Downsize, never discount
04:49 Lever 5 — Charge the invisible hours
05:43 Lever 6 — Price the outcome
06:34 Lever 7 — Sell the same build twice
07:25 Which one to pull this week

WHAT THIS IS

Seven mechanical changes to a quote and a sales call, ordered by time-to-cash rather than by
importance. Lever 1 shows up on your next invoice. Lever 7 takes a quarter and then compounds.

  1. The renewal letter — a rate rise you give notice of, not permission for.
  2. Sell the date — the same project, a shorter timeline, a higher price.
  3. Three prices beat one — anchoring, without the straw man.
  4. Downsize, never discount — remove scope, never the rate.
  5. Charge the invisible hours — the kickoff call is a deliverable.
  6. Price the outcome — fixed scope, capped revisions, no hourly punishment.
  7. Sell the same build twice — the thing you already made, sold again.

WHO IT IS FOR

Freelancers, consultants and one-person businesses who already have clients and already send
quotes. If you have never quoted a price, levers 1 and 3 will mean nothing to you yet — watch
levers 2 and 4 first.

WHAT IT ASSUMES

That you would rather lose one client than keep paying a discount you never agreed to. Every lever
here trades a little comfort for a better number, and not one of them needs a new follower, a new
lead source or another hour in the day.

NONE OF IT IS MINDSET

That is the point. Everything in the video is a change to a document or a sentence you say out
loud. Nothing in it asks you to feel differently about money first.
```
